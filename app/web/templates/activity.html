Reading the trace

Each line is one decision, in the order it was made.

ping A direct message it read, including ones the feed had buried.
llm What went to the local model, and what came back.
rule A decision made without the model — phrasing, dates, thresholds.
writeback Something it created in Basecamp under your account.
reply A draft written in your name — inert until you send it.
error Something failed. Nothing is dropped silently — it lands here.
