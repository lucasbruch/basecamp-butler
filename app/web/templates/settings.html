Who may be auto-replied to

An allowlist. Only these people ever get an answer, and each one carries its own tone. Answer in names the one Ping conversation a rule may speak in — a Ping isn't always a two-person chat, and a reply in your voice is read by everyone in the room, so you pick the room rather than let it be guessed. A rule with no conversation set says nothing at all. Before and after are pasted around the reply word for word, each on its own line — a sign-off, or a note that the message was drafted for you. Unlike the tone, they aren't a request the model can talk itself out of, and they go into the draft as it's written, so what you read on the Replies page is the whole message. Draft parks the reply on the Replies page for you to read and send; auto posts it to Basecamp by itself. Start on draft for a week — what a model writes in your voice is worth watching before you let it out.
Replies are written by the local LLM, so this needs a reachable Ollama at {{ settings.ollama_url }}. It doesn't depend on the classifier setting.
